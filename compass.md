# Genre Color Palette for UI Design

A curated color palette for movie and TV show genres, optimized for dark mode interfaces.

| Genre | HEX | Color Name | Recommended UI Background | Vibe & Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Action** | `#FF3B30` | Electric Red | `#18181B` (Carbon Black) | Adrenaline, speed, high impact, explosions |
| **Adventure** | `#10B981` | Emerald Green | `#061A12` (Dark Forest) | Exploration, nature, discoveries, journey |
| **Animation** | `#38BDF8` | Sky Blue | `#0F172A` (Steel Night) | Creativity, illustrated worlds, lightheartedness |
| **Comedy** | `#FACC15` | Bright Yellow | `#1C1917` (Warm Dark) | Humor, lightness, energy, positivity |
| **Crime / Noir** | `#64748B` | Slate Gray | `#09090B` (Absolute Black) | Mystery, investigation, shadows, cynicism |
| **Documentary** | `#D4AF37` | Antique Gold | `#141312` (Dark Sepia) | Reality, history, memory, authority |
| **Drama** | `#722F37` | Wine Red / Burgundy | `#1C1917` (Warm Charcoal) | Emotion, elegance, drama, intimacy |
| **Fantasy** | `#A855F7` | Mystic Purple | `#12091F` (Dark Purple) | Magic, spells, unknown worlds, epic scale |
| **Horror** | `#8B0000` | Dark Blood Red | `#0D0D0D` (Pitch Black) | Fear, danger, darkness, suspense |
| **Romance** | `#EC4899` | Deep Pink / Magenta | `#1F0B16` (Night Burgundy) | Passion, sweetness, relationships, intimacy |
| **Sci-Fi** | `#00D2FF` | Electric Cyan | `#0A1128` (Night Blue) | Technology, future, holograms, space |
| **Thriller** | `#D97706` | Amber Orange | `#0F172A` (Steel Blue) | Tension, suspicion, alert, high stakes |
